distribution of electromagnetic waves in terms 
of wavelength or frequency (Figure 5.10).
Radio waves
They are produced by accelerated 
motion of charges in conducting wires. The 
frequency range is from a few Hz to 109 Hz. 
They show reflection and diffraction.
They are used in radio and television 
communication systems and also in cellular 
phones to transmit voice communication 
in the ultra high frequency band. 
Microwaves

such as klystron, magnetron and gunndiode.

and can be polarised. 
It is used in radar system for aircraft 
navigation, speed of the vehicle, microwave 
oven for cooking and very long distance 
wireless communication through satellites.
